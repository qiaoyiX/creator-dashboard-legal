Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Creator Dashboard is a personal analytics tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner to view statistics for their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

When the owner connects an account, the app uses the official YouTube and TikTok APIs to read:

  • TikTok: display name, follower count, total likes, video count, and for each public video its title or description, publish time, duration, link, cover image, and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • YouTube: channel statistics, video details and statistics, and YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel.

Access is read-only. The app never posts, edits or deletes anything.

How data is stored and used

All data, including access tokens, is stored in a local database on the owner's computer. It is used only to display charts and statistics to the owner. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app, and no data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data and tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or from the Google account's third-party access page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
